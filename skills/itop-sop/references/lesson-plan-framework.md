# Lesson Plan Framework

Reference for Procedure 2 (Lesson Plan Creator).

---

## Learning Objective Types

All lesson plans must include at least one objective from each category:

| Type | Prefix | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Skill | [SKILL] | "Participant can demonstrate {observable action} on typodont" |
| Knowledge | [KNOWLEDGE] | "Participant can explain {concept} in their own words" |
| Attitude | [ATTITUDE] | "Participant acknowledges {motivational/professional stance}" |

Objectives must be observable and assessable. Avoid: "understands", "appreciates", "is aware of".
Use instead: "demonstrates", "identifies", "applies", "corrects", "explains to a peer".

---

## ODPF Time Ratios by Duration

See `touch-to-teach.md` for full ratios. Summary:

| Block | Min % of session |
|---|---|
| Observe + Demonstrate | 15% |
| Guided Practice | 25% |
| Independent Practice | 35% |
| Feedback & Debrief | 10% |
| Hook & Context | ≤15% (theory cap) |

Total hands-on (Guided + Independent Practice) ≥ 60%.

---

## Spaced Repetition Scheduling

Every lesson plan must include a spaced repetition note in the Feedback block — identifying which skill to revisit at the next session and at what interval.

| Session Gap | Repetition Guidance |
|---|---|
| Next session (≤1 week) | Re-check competency checkpoint from current session |
| 2–4 weeks | Brief re-demonstration + spot-check on typodont |
| > 4 weeks | Full ODPF cycle for the skill before advancing |

---

## Competency Gates

Competency gates are pass/fail checkpoints — not grades. Format:

```
Competency gate: {observable behaviour at pass level}
  e.g., "Demonstrates 45° bristle angulation on all 6 zones without correction"
Remediation: {specific corrective action if not met}
  e.g., "Instructor physically re-guides angulation on zones 1–2; re-attempt zones 1–6"
```

Never advance a learner to patient work without a documented competency gate pass.

---

## Motivational Prompts for Instructors

Use these at the Hook and Feedback blocks:

- "Tell me about a patient where this made a real difference..." (patient story anchor)
- "What did you notice in your hands when you adjusted the angle?" (proprioceptive reflection)
- "What would you do differently in zone 3 next time?" (deliberate practice framing)
- "You improved your angulation in zones 4–6 — did you feel that?" (micro-improvement celebration)

## Series Sequencing (Multi-Session Curriculum)

For multi-session lesson plan series:

| Session | Focus | Prerequisite |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to iTOP philosophy + Bass technique zones 1–3 | None |
| 2 | Bass technique zones 4–6 + interdental aid introduction | Session 1 competency gate passed |
| 3 | Full-mouth Bass + BOB measurement introduction | Session 2 competency gate passed |
| 4 | BOB-guided intervention planning | Session 3 competency gate passed |
| 5+ | Advanced technique refinement + patient communication | Session 4 competency gate passed |
