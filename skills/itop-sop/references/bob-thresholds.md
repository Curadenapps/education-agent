# BOB Thresholds & iTOP Intervention Mapping

Source: iTOP Guidelines 2024 + BOB iTOP Positioning Brief
Always defer to the supervising clinician for individual patient interpretation.

## Metrics

- **BOB%** — Bleeding on Brushing percentage
- **PCR%** — Plaque Control Record percentage
- **MGI** — Modified Gingival Index (0–3 scale)

## Threshold Table

| Metric | Value | iTOP Tier | Intervention Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| BOB% | < 10% | Maintenance | Reinforce current technique; consider recall extension |
| BOB% | 10–25% | Improvement | Technique correction (Modified Bass); interdental review |
| BOB% | > 25% | Intensive | Full iTOP re-introduction; increase session frequency |
| PCR% | < 10% | Maintenance | No change required |
| PCR% | 10–30% | Improvement | Disclosure agent use; motivational interviewing focus |
| PCR% | > 30% | Intensive | Supervised brushing; step-by-step re-training |
| MGI | 0–1 | Healthy tissue | Monitor longitudinally |
| MGI | 2–3 | Inflamed tissue | Flag for clinician — may indicate systemic factors |

## Trend Interpretation

| Trend | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| improving | BOB/PCR decreasing across sessions | Positive reinforcement; maintain frequency |
| stable | No significant change | Review technique compliance; motivational check |
| worsening | BOB/PCR increasing | Escalate tier; consider more frequent recall |
| first session | No baseline to compare | Establish baseline; educate on scoring |

## Combined Risk Flags

- BOB% > 25% AND MGI 2–3 → Intensive tier + immediate clinician referral flag
- PCR% > 30% AND BOB% > 25% → Full iTOP re-introduction + supervised session required
- All metrics in Maintenance tier for 3+ consecutive sessions → Consider extended recall interval (clinician decision)

## Guardrail

These thresholds are informational guides only. No clinical diagnosis may be made from these values. All tier escalation decisions require `[CLINICIAN REVIEW REQUIRED]`.
