## iTOP Info

- **Role:** clinician | educator
- **Topic:** iTOP philosophy and methods overview
- **Format:** lecture (in-person)
- **Duration:** up to 90 minutes
- **Level:** beginner
- **URL:** www.curadenacademy.com
- **Summary:** Single introductory lecture for larger groups of dental professionals. Covers iTOP methods and philosophy only. Does NOT count as an official seminar and cannot replace any formal iTOP level.
- **Tags:** itop intro, philosophy, overview, dental professionals, lecture

## iTOP Introductory

- **ID:** itop-introductory
- **Role:** clinician | educator
- **Topic:** Biofilm management, Bass technique, IDB, solo technique, loop floss, patient motivation
- **Format:** in-person (1 day)
- **Duration:** 1 day (approx. 8 hours) | CPD credits: country-dependent
- **Level:** beginner
- **Price:** 150–300 € (country-specific)
- **URL:** www.curadenacademy.com
- **Summary:** Foundational iTOP seminar. 2–3 hours theory + 3 hours hands-on T2T practice. Covers biofilm management, iTOP prophylaxis system, and hands-on technique training (IDB, Bass, Solo, Loop floss). Max T2T ratio 1:8.
- **Tags:** introductory, bass technique, idb, solo technique, loop floss, t2t, patient education, biofilm

## iTOP Advanced

- **ID:** itop-advanced
- **Role:** clinician | educator
- **Topic:** Deeper implementation, 1:1 patient instruction, iTOP as a business model
- **Format:** in-person (2 days)
- **Duration:** 2 days | CPD credits: country-dependent
- **Level:** intermediate
- **Prerequisite:** iTOP Introductory recommended (not mandatory)
- **Price:** 300–500 € (accommodation not included)
- **URL:** www.curadenacademy.com
- **Summary:** Two-day deep-dive into iTOP implementation. Each participant receives 1:1 time with an iTOP Instructor. Max T2T ratio 1:6. Includes participant-to-participant instruction under supervision.
- **Tags:** advanced, 1:1 training, implementation, t2t, patient instruction, instructor

## iTOP Recall

- **ID:** itop-recall
- **Role:** clinician | educator
- **Topic:** Annual skill recalibration — technique refresh, scientific update, practice motivation
- **Format:** in-person (half day)
- **Duration:** Half day (approx. 4 hours) | 1:2 or 1:1 T2T setting
- **Level:** intermediate | advanced
- **Prerequisite:** Previous iTOP participation
- **Price:** 50–100 € or free of charge
- **URL:** www.curadenacademy.com
- **Summary:** Annual refresher for certified professionals. Half theory, 1 hour practical in 1:2 or 1:1 setting. Mandatory yearly for all Lecturers and Instructors. Also available as thematic Recall (Orthodontics, Implants, Periodontology, Gerodontology, Paediatrics, Xerostomia).
- **Tags:** recall, refresher, annual, recalibration, t2t, instructor, lecturer, mandatory

---

## iTOP Educator

- **ID:** itop-educator
- **Role:** educator
- **Topic:** Becoming an iTOP Instructor — T2T mastery, teaching methodology
- **Format:** in-person (4 days)
- **Duration:** 4 days | intensive 1:2 T2T with daily 1:1 sessions
- **Level:** advanced
- **Prerequisite:** 2 full days of iTOP training with an Instructor (within last 2 years) + recommendation from local partner or iTOP Lecturer
- **URL:** Contact Academy Department — must be coordinated in advance
- **Summary:** Four-day intensive programme for dental professionals wishing to become certified iTOP Instructors. Intense 1:2 T2T with high-level Instructors including 1:1 daily. Must be planned in coordination with the HQ Academy Department.
- **Tags:** educator, instructor certification, t2t mastery, certification pathway, 4 days
