# iTOP Operational Knowledge

Source: iTOP Guidelines 2024
Confidential — internal use only. Last updated: 31.07.2024

---

## Staff Roles

### General Requirements (All iTOP Staff)
- Dental professional actively working with patients on a regular basis
- Highly developed practical T2T competence
- Teaching passion and proficiency
- Not currently employed by CURADEN AG (exceptions by Education Department only)

### iTOP Instructor
- Responsible for the **practical T2T part only**
- Awarded *Certificate of iTOP Instructor* after completing iTOP Educator seminar
- Must have attended Introductory and Advanced before Educator
- Must complete yearly Recall in their country to maintain status
- Can hold lectures on iTOP-related topics but **cannot run regular iTOP seminars independently**

### iTOP Lecturer
- Responsible for **both theoretical and practical parts**
- Requirements: certified Instructor → minimum 2 official seminar instructions → recommendation → HQ Academy approval → contract signing
- Process: HQ assigns mentor → presentation preparation → presentation to HQ team → approval → contract → first seminar with mentor
- Must complete yearly Recall
- **Basic iTOP team = 1 Lecturer + 2 Instructors**

### Sales Representatives
- Cannot work as iTOP Instructors unless they are dental professionals actively working with patients

## Education Levels

### iTOP Info
- Single lecture, max 90 minutes
- For larger groups of dental professionals
- Introduction to iTOP methods and philosophy only
- **Does NOT count as an official seminar**
- Cannot replace Introductory or any other level
- Can be held by Lecturer or Instructor

### iTOP Introductory (1 day)
- Format: 2–3 hours theory + 3 hours T2T practical
- T2T ratio: **max 1:8** (do not exceed under any circumstances)
- Minimum staff: 1 iTOP Lecturer

**Standard Agenda:**
| Time | Content |
|---|---|
| 08:45 | Welcome |
| 09:00 | Theory Part 1 — Why manage biofilm, daily routine |
| 11:00 | Coffee break |
| 11:15 | Theory Part 2 — Biofilm criteria, patient motivation |
| 12:00 | Lunch |
| 13:30 | T2T — Interdental brushes, loop floss |
| 15:00 | Coffee break |
| 15:15 | T2T — Bass technique, solo technique, Hydrosonic demo |
| 16:45 | Main points & discussion |
| 17:00 | Evaluation, certification, group photo |
| 17:15 | End |

### iTOP Advanced (2 days)
- Format: up to 3 hours theory + 3 hours T2T per day
- T2T ratio: **max 1:6**; includes 1:1 time with Instructor for each participant
- Minimum staff: **at least 2 iTOP Lecturers**
- Participants instruct each other under Instructor supervision

### iTOP Recall (half day)
- Format: ½ theory + 1 hour practical
- T2T: 1:2 or 1:1 setting
- Annual — mandatory for all Lecturers and Instructors
- Also available as thematic Recall (Ortho, Implant, Perio, Gero, Paediatrics, Xerostomia)

### iTOP Educator (4 days)
- For professionals wishing to become Instructors
- Intense 1:2 T2T with high-level Instructors; includes 1:1 daily
- Requirements: 2 full days iTOP training with an Instructor (within last 2 years) + local partner/Lecturer reference
- Must be coordinated with Academy Department

### T2T Ratio Summary

| Level | Max Ratio | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Introductory | 1:8 | Hard limit — never exceed |
| Advanced | 1:6 | Each participant gets 1:1 time |
| Educator | 1:2 and 1:1 | Daily throughout programme |
| Recall | 1:2 or 1:1 | Skill recalibration setting |

## Recall System

### For Regular Professionals
- General Recall: theory + 1:1 T2T
- Thematic Recall: topic-specific theory + 1:1 T2T
- Aims: professional relations, scientific update, skill recalibration, networking

### For Instructors
- Must complete Recall with local Lecturer at least once a year
- Team must be recalibrated before each local seminar
- If Instructor level is unsatisfactory, Lecturer decides whether to swap out or invest in further Recall

### For Lecturers
- Recall done with another Lecturer (visiting international or at annual Symposium)
- Lecturer's duty: conduct Recall for local iTOP team once a year, **free of charge**
- **Mandatory: all Lecturers and Instructors must complete T2T Recall yearly**

## Annual Seminar Requirements

**With local Lecturer:**
- Minimum 3 Introductory seminars (1 day each) + 1 Advanced (2 days) per year

**Without local Lecturer:**
- Minimum 2 Introductory OR 1 Introductory + 1 Advanced (with international support)
- Contact Academy Department if minimum standards cannot be met

## Seminar Planning: Before / During / After

### Before
- Inform iTOP team and Academy Department (venue, Lecturer, Instructors)
- **Create and publish seminar on curadenacademy.com at least 4 months in advance**
- Book hotel, room, catering (buffet preferred, max 2 menu options)
- Arrange: projector, microphone, translator if needed
- Brief Lecturer, Instructors, and sales rep on schedule and participant info
- Import any participants who did not self-register
- Print certificates via curadenacademy.com
- Send programme to participants (address, parking, schedule, contact)
- Prepare: name/table cards, evaluation forms, iTOP materials, participation list

### During
- Room setup: iTOP banners, participant seats, T2T island tables, product display
- Quick introduction: Lecturer, Instructors, agenda
- Theoretical session by Lecturer
- T2T sessions at correct ratios
- Group photo with CS 5460 demo brush (get photo permission)
- Evaluation and certification at close

### After
- Analyse seminar; share evaluation with teaching team
- Discuss feedback and improvement ideas
- Send thank-you emails to all participants
- Activate sales reps for follow-up visits **1–2 weeks after seminar**

## Room Setup

**Theory session:** U-shape seating. Each seat: iTOP notebook + pen. Print and display agenda.

**T2T practical session:** Island tables at correct ratio spacing. Good lighting is essential. Adequate space between islands.

## Rights & Responsibilities

### Curaden Partner
- Plans annual education strategy with AM and Academy Department
- Ensures Lecturer undergoes yearly Recall
- Organises Recalls for Instructor team and general professionals
- Uses marketing materials per official iTOP branding only
- Technical details in cooperation with Lecturer
- Reports any irregularities to Academy Department

### Local iTOP Lecturer
- Attends yearly Recall
- Ensures quality of lectures per Curaden Academy Guidelines
- Updates presentation (pictures, products, names) — changes to Master Presentation require HQ approval
- Conducts yearly Recall for local Instructors, free of charge
- **Keeps iTOP strictly independent from CURAPROX — iTOP is NOT product training**
- Reports availability for local events
- Reports irregularities to Academy Department

### Academy Department
- Sets global standards for professional education
- Assigns international Lecturers on request
- Updates website with international events
- **Solely authorised to ban seminars if standards not met**
- Certifies all Lecturers and Instructors
- Cannot be bypassed for certification, presentation changes, or logo use

---

## Hard Operational Rules

1. **iTOP is NOT product training.** Never conflate with CURAPROX promotion. Gently correct if a user conflates them.
2. **Minimum ratios are mandatory.** Flag clearly if a planned event exceeds T2T ratios.
3. **Master Presentation changes require Academy Department approval** before use.
4. **All iTOP seminars must be published on curadenacademy.com** — events must go live at least 4 months ahead.
5. **Certificates must come from curadenacademy.com.** Users cannot create their own.
6. **Sales reps cannot be iTOP Instructors** unless dental professionals actively working with patients.
7. **Shorter events not meeting minimum criteria cannot be called iTOP.**
8. **Events with different content cannot be called iTOP.**

---

## Logo & Brand Rules

- iTOP logo may only be used on iTOP marketing materials
- Can be used together with the Curaden Academy brand
- **DO NOT MIX iTOP and CURAPROX branding**
- iTOP should look professionally independent — connection to CURAPROX should be clear but not dominant
- Locally produced material requires Academy Department approval before distribution
