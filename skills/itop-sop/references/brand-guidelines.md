## Brand Identity

**iTOP** is Curaden's education platform for oral health professionals. It bridges science and daily clinical practice — helping professionals become educators, coaches, and trusted partners in their patients' health journey.

The four pillars behind the name:

| Letter | Word | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| i | Individually | Every patient is unique and deserves a tailored approach |
| T | Trained | True prevention comes through guided, hands-on training |
| O | Oral | The mouth is the gateway to overall health |
| P | Prophylaxis | Our goal is to maintain health through prevention |

**Founder quote (approved for use):**
> "Dear patient, let me be your teacher. Otherwise, I'll be fixing your teeth forever."
> — MUDr. Jiří Sedelmayer, Founder of iTOP

**Signature phrases (approved for use in materials):**
- "Prevention isn't a theory. It's a practice."
- "Knowledge alone isn't enough."
- "Touch to Teach — learn by doing."

## Voice & Tone

iTOP speaks the way a skilled coach speaks — confident, clear, and human. We teach; we don't lecture.

**We are:**
- Evidence-informed, not dogmatic
- Plain-spoken and Swiss-direct
- Warm, respectful, human
- Action-oriented — acceptance, atraumaticity, efficacy
- Patient-centred, never paternalistic

**We avoid:**
- Jargon or academic distance
- Marketing superlatives ("revolutionary", "world-class")
- Fear-based messaging
- Prescriptive command language
- Generic stock wellness clichés

## Logo & Mark

The iTOP mark is a geometric wordmark — rectangular blocks, uniform stroke weight, anchored by a shared top bar. A small square dot floats above the `i`.

**Approved colour variants:**
| Variant | Use case |
|---|---|
| iTOP Cyan on White | Primary — light backgrounds |
| White on Deep Navy | Reversed — dark backgrounds |
| White on iTOP Cyan | Cyan backgrounds |

**Logo rules:**
- Clear space = cap-height of the `T` on all sides
- Minimum size: 16 px digital / 10 mm print
- The dot above the `i` must always be a perfect square
- **Never** re-typeset, re-draw, recolour, distort, or place on a busy background
- Use only official SVG/EPS/PNG files from the Brand team (brand@curaden.ch)

**Logo misuse (hard prohibitions):**
- ❌ Recolour the mark
- ❌ Distort proportions
- ❌ Use below minimum size or at reduced opacity
- ❌ Place on busy or photographic backgrounds

## Typography

**Primary typeface:** Galaxie Polaris Book (weight 400)
**Licence:** Village Type
**Fallback:** Work Sans (system sans-serif)

One family across all iTOP communications — print, presentations, digital.

| Role | Spec |
|---|---|
| Display | Book · 56–80 px · –2% tracking · 0.95 line-height |
| Headline | Book · 28–36 px · –1% tracking · 1.1 line-height |
| Body | Book · 15–17 px · 1.55 line-height |
| Caption | Book · 12–13 px · 1.45 line-height |
| Eyebrow | Book · 11 px · +18% tracking · UPPERCASE |

**Typography rules:**
- Never substitute an alternative typeface without Brand approval
- Eyebrow labels (e.g. course designation labels) are always uppercase with wide tracking
- Do not use bold weights for body text — visual hierarchy is achieved through size and spacing

## Course Designation System

Every iTOP artifact belongs to exactly one course track. The designation colour is applied to:
- The feature bubble on title slides
- Chapter divider bars
- Certificate seals
- Pills on social cards

**Rules:**
- Never use more than one designation colour in a single artifact
- The designation accent is always the minority colour (≈6% of total surface)
- Everything else stays in the Navy / Lime / White spine

---

## Bubble System

The bubble is iTOP's signature background element — a nod to biofilm, droplets, and clean circular geometry. Every PowerPoint, document, and poster uses the same bubble grammar.

### Four templated layouts

| Template | Use |
|---|---|
| A | Title slide — Introductory (Lime accent) |
| B | Title slide — Advanced (Cyan accent) |
| C | Title slide — Recall (Coral accent) |
| D | Section divider — Dark (Navy background) |

### Bubble rules

**Do:**
- One large Navy bubble anchored to an edge
- One designation-coloured bubble for focus
- One small satellite bubble for rhythm
- Let bubbles bleed off the page — composition should feel calm and unforced
- Keep bubbles perfectly circular with flat fills

**Don't:**
- Use more than 4 bubbles per layout
- Mix designation colours in one layout
- Outline the bubbles — always flat fills
- Animate bubbles as loops in decks — static only
- Place type behind bubbles — headlines always in the open quadrant

## Imagery & Illustration

**Photography direction:**
- Show real professionals teaching patients, hands demonstrating technique, tools in use
- Natural, diffuse light — uncluttered clinical settings
- Swiss restraint — no staged stock smiles, over-retouched skin, or glowing-teeth aesthetics
- Never use fear-based or clinical-horror imagery

**Technical illustrations:**
- Use brand palette — Navy line work with Lime, Cyan, or Coral fills
- Flat fills, confident line weights, no gradients or drop shadows
- Source: official illustration files (Chapters I–IV available from Brand team)

## Artifact Standards

Every iTOP artifact is built on:
1. The bubble grammar (templates A–D)
2. Galaxie Polaris typography
3. The White / Navy / Lime colour spine
4. One designation accent marking the course track

### Standard artifact types

**Digital:** Slide decks, social quote cards, workbook covers, learning platform modules, email templates, LinkedIn banners

**Print:** Certificates of completion, seminar flyers (A5), wall posters, roll-up banners, name badges, notebooks, tote bags (1-colour screen print — Lime on Navy)

### Digital channel standards

| Channel | Standard |
|---|---|
| Learning platform | Cyan progress indicators; Lime for completion |
| Email | Navy-on-white template; single designation CTA; no decorative imagery above fold |
| LinkedIn & Social | Navy-dominant; bubbles always on; peer-led tone (quotes, technique clips, recaps) |

## Pre-Publish Checklist

Before any artifact goes to print or publish:

- [ ] Uses official iTOP SVG/EPS wordmark — not re-typeset
- [ ] Galaxie Polaris Book — no substitute fonts
- [ ] Palette: White, Navy, Lime + one designation accent only
- [ ] Colour ratio: White 50 / Navy 30 / Lime 14 / Designation 6
- [ ] Bubble grammar present — 2–4 circles, one Navy anchor
- [ ] Only one designation colour per artifact
- [ ] Imagery is authentic — no stock smiles or clinical horror
- [ ] Voice is practical, peer-to-peer, never promotional
- [ ] Proofed by Brand & Education before distribution

## Local Adaptation Rules

**Permitted locally:**
- Translate body copy into local language
- Swap imagery for local clinicians and settings
- Adjust seminar dates, venues, prices
- Add local sponsor logos in a clear sponsor lockup

**Always controlled centrally:**
- iTOP logo & wordmark files
- Palette hex / CMYK definitions
- Galaxie Polaris licence & font files
- Master PowerPoint & certificate templates
- Bubble templates A–D
- Founder quotes & signature phrases
