## 1. The Brand

**iTOP — You, Your Team, Your Patient.**

iTOP is Curaden's education platform for oral health professionals. It bridges the gap between science and daily clinical practice — helping professionals become educators, coaches and trusted partners in their patients' health journey.

> *"Dear patient, let me be your teacher. Otherwise, I'll be fixing your teeth forever."*  
> — MUDr. Jiří Sedelmayer, Founder of iTOP

### The Four Pillars (the name, literally)

| Letter | Word | Meaning |
|--------|------|---------|
| **i** | Individually | Every patient is unique and deserves a tailored approach |
| **T** | Trained | True prevention comes through guided, hands-on training |
| **O** | Oral | The mouth is the gateway to overall health |
| **P** | Prophylaxis | Our goal is to maintain health through prevention |

## 2. Voice & Tone

**Practical, personal, grounded.**

iTOP speaks the way a skilled coach speaks — confident, clear, and human. We teach, we don't lecture. We translate clinical evidence into language a professional can use with a patient tomorrow morning.

### We Are
- Evidence-informed, not dogmatic
- Plain-spoken and Swiss-direct
- Warm, respectful, human
- Action-oriented — acceptance, atraumaticity, efficacy
- Patient-centred, never paternalistic

### We Avoid
- Jargon or academic distance
- Marketing superlatives ("revolutionary", "world-class")
- Fear-based messaging
- Prescriptive command language
- Generic stock wellness clichés

### Signature Phrases
- "Prevention isn't a theory. It's a practice."
- "Knowledge alone isn't enough."
- "Touch to Teach — learn by doing."

## 3. Logo & Mark

**The iTOP geometric wordmark.**

The iTOP mark is constructed from geometric blocks — all rectangular, all the same stroke weight, anchored by a shared top bar. A small square dot floats above the *i*.

### Logo Files (canonical sources)

All logo files live at `assets/logos/` in this repository. Always use these files. **Never re-draw or re-typeset from scratch.**

| File | Use case |
|------|----------|
| `iTOP_Logo_RGB_Positive.svg` | Primary — cyan on white/light backgrounds |
| `iTOP_Logo_RGB_Negative.svg` | Reversed — white on navy/dark backgrounds |
| `iTOP_Logo_CMYK_ohne_Claim.svg` | Print — CMYK, no claim text, with circle |
| `iTOP_Logo_CMYK_ohne_Claim_ohne_kreis.svg` | Print — CMYK, no claim text, no circle |

PNG and EPS variants are available from the Brand team (brand@curaden.ch).

### Colour Rules
- On **white or light grey** backgrounds → use the **Cyan (positive)** logo
- On **Navy** backgrounds → use the **White (negative)** logo
- On **iTOP Cyan** backgrounds → use the **White (negative)** logo

### Clear Space
Clear space = cap-height of the T on all sides. Nothing enters this zone.

### Minimum Size
- **16 px** — digital
- **10 mm** — print

The dot above the *i* must always be a perfect square.

### Logo Misuse — Never Do These
- Recolour the mark
- Distort or stretch
- Use below minimum size or at low opacity
- Place on a busy photographic background
- Outline the mark
- Re-typeset from a system font

## 4. Typography

**Galaxie Polaris — Book.**

Galaxie Polaris Book is the body typeface for all iTOP communications — print, presentations, digital. One family across weights and sizes gives iTOP its calm, clinical consistency.

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Family | Galaxie Polaris |
| Weight | Book · 400 |
| Role | Body & default |
| Licence | Village Type |
| Fallback | Work Sans · system sans |

### Type Scale

| Role | Spec |
|------|------|
| **Display** | Book · 56–80 px · −2% tracking · 0.95 lh |
| **Headline** | Book · 28–36 px · −1% tracking · 1.1 lh |
| **Body** | Book · 15–17 px · 1.55 lh |
| **Caption** | Book · 12–13 px · 1.45 lh |
| **Eyebrow** | Book · 11 px · +18% tracking · UPPERCASE |

Mono companion: **JetBrains Mono** — used for eyebrows, labels, code, hex values.

## 5. Colour Palette

**White, Navy, Lime — in that order.**

White is the primary brand surface. Deep Navy carries the structural weight of the mark. iTOP Lime is the signature accent. Cool Grey and Pale Blue provide breathing room.

### Primary Palette

| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | Role |
|------|-----|-----|------|------|
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | 255 · 255 · 255 | — | Primary surface |
| **Deep Navy** | `#0E2841` | 14 · 40 · 65 | 100 · 78 · 45 · 45 | Mark, text, bubbles |
| **iTOP Lime** | `#BED243` | 190 · 210 · 67 | 30 · 0 · 85 · 0 | Signature accent |
| **Cool Grey** | `#E8E8E8` | 232 · 232 · 232 | — | Slide backgrounds |
| **Pale Blue** | `#F1F4FB` | 241 · 244 · 251 | — | Soft bubble fill, nested surfaces |
| **Sunflower** | `#FFDA5E` | 255 · 218 · 94 | — | Highlight, illustration fill |

### Course Designation Colours

One per track. Use only the designation colour for the feature bubble on title slides, the chapter bar, the certificate seal, and the pill on social cards.

| Track | Name | HEX | RGB |
|-------|------|-----|-----|
| **Introductory** | Lime · Foundation | `#BED243` | 190 · 210 · 67 |
| **Advanced** | Cyan · Deepening | `#00AED8` | 0 · 174 · 216 |
| **Recall** | Coral · Maintenance | `#FF6D6C` | 255 · 109 · 108 |

### Co-branding / System Colours

| Name | HEX | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| **Curaden Red** | `#BD0045` | Parent brand — co-branded use only |
| **Alert Red** | `#E1000A` | System warning |

### Recommended Colour Ratio

| Colour | Proportion |
|--------|-----------|
| White | 50% |
| Navy | 30% |
| Lime | 14% |
| Designation accent | 6% |

### Usage at a Glance
- **White** — primary surface for slides, documents, digital screens
- **Navy** — mark, text, primary anchor bubbles, dark backgrounds
- **Lime** — accent bubbles, Intro designation, highlights
- **Cool Grey** — slide background only; never use as body text colour
- **Cyan** — Advanced designation, current-state accents
- **Coral** — Recall designation, attention markers

## 7. Bubble System

**Circles are how iTOP breathes.**

The bubble is the signature background element — a nod to biofilm, droplets, clean circular geometry. Every PowerPoint, document, and poster uses the same bubble grammar.

### Four Templated Layouts

| Template | Use | Background | Description |
|----------|-----|-----------|-------------|
| **A** | Title slide — Introductory | Cool Grey | Large navy anchor (left/bottom), Lime feature bubble (right/top), tiny Lime satellite |
| **B** | Title slide — Advanced | White | Large navy anchor (right/top), Cyan feature bubble (left/bottom), tiny Cyan satellite |
| **C** | Title slide — Recall | Cool Grey | Large navy anchor (right/bottom), Coral feature bubble (left/top), tiny Coral satellite |
| **D** | Section divider — Dark | Navy | Lime accent bubble (right/bottom), Pale Blue ghost bubble (left/top) |

### Rules of the System

**Do:**
- One large Navy bubble anchored to an edge · one designation bubble for focus · one tiny satellite for rhythm
- Let bubbles bleed off the page — composition should feel calm and unforced
- Keep bubbles perfectly circular, flat fills — no shadows or gradients
- Place type in the open quadrant; bubbles never sit behind headlines

**Don't:**
- Use more than 4 bubbles per layout
- Mix designation colours in one layout
- Outline the bubbles — always flat fills
- Animate bubbles as loops in decks — static only

## 8. Imagery & Illustration

**Real people, real practice.**

iTOP imagery shows the work: professionals teaching patients, hands demonstrating technique, tools in use. Natural light, uncluttered scenes, Swiss restraint. Technical illustrations use the brand palette — navy line work with lime, cyan, or coral fills.

### Do
- Natural, diffuse light · authentic clinical settings · hands-in-action framing
- Illustrations in brand palette: flat fills, confident line weights

### Don't
- Staged stock smiles, over-retouched skin, glowing teeth
- Fear-based or clinical-horror imagery

Every artifact is built on the same bubble grammar, Galaxie Polaris type, and the White / Navy / Lime spine. Designation accents mark the track; everything else stays constant.

### Digital Artifacts

- LinkedIn banner

### Print Collateral
- Certificate of Completion
- Seminar flyer (A5)
- Wall poster / roll-up banner
- Technique card
- Patient leaflet
- Name badge
- Notebook
- Tote bag (1-colour, Lime on Navy)

### Channels

| Channel | Notes |
|---------|-------|

## 10. For Education Managers

### Pre-Publish Checklist

Before anything goes to print or publish, confirm:

- [ ] Uses official iTOP SVG/EPS wordmark (not re-typeset)
- [ ] Galaxie Polaris Book — no substitute fonts
- [ ] Palette: White, Navy, Lime + one designation accent only
- [ ] Colour ratio: White 50 / Navy 30 / Lime 14 / Designation 6
- [ ] Bubble grammar present — 2–4 circles, one Navy anchor
- [ ] Only one designation colour per artifact
- [ ] Imagery is authentic — no stock smiles or clinical horror
- [ ] Voice is practical, peer-to-peer, never promotional
- [ ] Proofed by Brand & Education before distribution

### Local Flexibility (Permitted)
- Translate body copy into local language
- Swap imagery for local clinicians and settings
- Adjust seminar dates, venues, prices
- Add local sponsor logos in a clear sponsor lockup

### Always Central (Never Localise)
- iTOP logo and wordmark files
- Palette hex / CMYK definitions
- Galaxie Polaris licence and font files
- Master PowerPoint and certificate templates
- Bubble templates A–D
- Founder quotes and signature phrases

### Asset Library (Request from Brand)
- Logo artwork (SVG · EPS · PNG) — see also `assets/logos/` in this repo
- Galaxie Polaris fonts
- PowerPoint master
- Bubble templates A–D
- Certificate template
- Technical illustrations — Chapters I–IV
- Book cover artwork
- Course descriptions
