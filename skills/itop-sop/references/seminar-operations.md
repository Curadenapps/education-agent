## The Curaden Academy Customer Journey

The iTOP seminar sits within a broader six-stage customer journey. Understanding the full journey helps iTOP staff and partners position the seminar correctly and execute each stage effectively.

| Stage | What happens |
|-------|-------------|
| **Awareness** | Dental professional (DP) learns about iTOP via sales rep, website, email, WOM, or paid search |
| **Consideration** | DP evaluates the course — reviews website, flyer, asks questions, checks price and schedule |
| **Purchase** | DP registers and pays; receives booking confirmation, invoice, payment confirmation, reminder 2 weeks prior |
| **Participation** | DP attends the seminar — quality of theory, T2T, networking, and experience |
| **Reflecting** | DP processes what they learned; receives implementation guide and follow-up contact |
| **Implementing** | DP applies iTOP in their clinic; sales rep and instructor follow up |
| **Follow-up / Recall** | DP returns for Recall or next-level seminar |

**Key KPIs per stage:**
- Awareness: how did they hear about the course ratio (optimal mix across channels)
- Consideration: avg response time, avg questions before registration
- Purchase: registration rate from direct contacts, payment confirmation time
- Participation: evaluation form ratings, voucher activation
- Implementing: % of participants implementing iTOP philosophy post-seminar
- Follow-up: post-seminar visit completion rate within 1–2 weeks

## Before the Seminar

### Planning timeline

| Checkpoint | Tasks |
|-----------|-------|
| T-4 months+ | Create and publish seminar on curadenacademy.com — **mandatory, no exceptions** |
| T-4 weeks | Confirm venue, participant count, equipment inventory, facilitator assignments. Inform iTOP team and Academy Department (venue, Lecturer, Instructors) |
| T-1 week | Send pre-reading and programme to participants (address, parking, schedule, contact). Confirm equipment deliveries. Print handouts. Brief Lecturer, Instructors, and sales rep on schedule and participant info |
| T-1 day | Set up room. Brief facilitators on station rotations. Import participants not self-registered |
| Day-of | Arrive 60 min early. Final station check. Print certificates via curadenacademy.com. Participant registration. Welcome brief |

### Room setup — Theory session
U-shape seating. Each seat: iTOP notebook + pen. Print and display agenda. Room branding: iTOP banners. Check projector, microphone, temperature, lighting, accessibility.

### Room setup — T2T practical session
Island tables at correct ratio spacing. Good lighting is essential. Adequate space between islands. Each station: typodont/phantom, instrument kit, disclosure tablets, handout.

### Required seminar materials
**Core products — seminars cannot be conducted without all of these:**
- CS 5460, CS smart, CS 12460 velvet, CS 1006, CS 1009
- CPS prime, CPS perio
- DF 834 dental floss
- Hydrosonic Pro

**Required items per seminar:**
- iTOP cosmetic bag for T2T incl. products (No. 73345035) OR T2T Triangle (No. 73345169)
- iTOP Notebook (No. 73300271 EN) — can be printed locally from Brand Hub
- Hydrosonic Pro Trials (No. 73349236)
- Pens in CPS prime and CPS perio colours (buy locally)

Use the Excel Material Calculator on Brand Hub for exact quantities.

## During the Seminar

### Standard sequence
1. Quick introduction: Lecturer, Instructors, agenda overview
2. Theoretical session(s) by Lecturer
3. T2T sessions at correct ratios
4. Active learning blocks (Advanced only)
5. Group photo with CS 5460 demo brush — get photo permission
6. Evaluation and certification at close

### Participant engagement — opening the seminar
Multiple interactive tools can be used to open and orient participants:
- Mentimeter.com — live polling to surface prior knowledge and set expectations
- Sticky notes — surfacing concerns or questions before the first lecture
- Balloons / Lego / physical props — making abstract concepts tangible
- Already defined tables with mixed groups — prevents clustering by prior relationship

### Managing the room
- Sales rep participates as a regular participant — not as a presenter
- Each participant had opportunity to participate in active discussion
- Each participant had opportunity to ask questions during theory blocks
- Clean working space and available tools at each T2T station
- T2T with each participant — no one skipped

### T2T ratio enforcement
| Level | Max ratio | Hard rule |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Introductory | 1:6 (T2T) | Never exceed — this is a guidelines violation |
| Advanced | 1:6 (T2T) | Each participant must receive 1:1 time |
| Recall | 1:2 or 1:1 | Skill recalibration setting |
| Educator | 1:2 and 1:1 | Daily throughout programme |

**If a planned event exceeds T2T ratios, flag immediately:** "This exceeds the maximum ratio — you need X instructors for Y participants."

## After the Seminar

### Immediate close
- Evaluation and certification at close — certificates from curadenacademy.com only
- Group photo
- Call to action for next events
- Starter Kit / product gift: IDB Essential Box (No. 73345035) or equivalent — gives participants immediate product to try and creates refill relationship

### Post-seminar follow-up

**1–2 weeks after seminar:**
- Sales rep conducts personal visit to each participant
- Divide attendees among sales reps (individual ownership)
- Segment participants by category (level of interest and decision-making power)

**3–4 weeks after seminar:**
- Instructor follow-up via email to support implementation
- Triangle of engagement: Sales Rep — Client — Instructor must be maintained

**Automated / system steps:**
- Send customised "Thank you" email with follow-up steps
- Track voucher activation
- Track open rate of thank-you email

**Ongoing:**
- Document all follow-ups in CRM (HubSpot or equivalent)
- Analyse seminar; share evaluation with teaching team
- Discuss feedback and improvement ideas

### Pricing guidelines
| Seminar | Recommended Price |
|---------|-----------------|
| iTOP Introductory (1 day) | 150–300 € |
| iTOP Advanced (2 days) | 300–500 € (accommodation not included) |
| iTOP Recall (professionals) | 50–100 € or free of charge |

- iTOP should never be completely free except for key individuals or strategic relationships
- Fees should cover at least costs, with transparent communication on any subsidised portions
- At universities, iTOP Introductory sessions can be free to encourage adoption
- Offer group discounts for practices sending multiple people
- Chairside gift after Introductory sessions: IDB Essential Box — creates incentive for product refills

## Annual Seminar Requirements

**With local Lecturer:**
- Minimum 3 Introductory seminars (1 day each) + 1 Advanced (2 days) per year

**Without local Lecturer:**
- Minimum 2 Introductory OR 1 Introductory + 1 Advanced (with international support)
- Contact Academy Department if minimum standards cannot be met

## Hard Operational Rules

1. **iTOP is NOT product training.** Never conflate with CURAPROX promotion.
2. **Minimum ratios are mandatory.** Flag clearly if a planned event exceeds T2T ratios.
3. **Master Presentation changes require Academy Department approval** before use.
4. **All iTOP seminars must be published on curadenacademy.com** — at least 4 months in advance.
5. **Certificates must come from curadenacademy.com.** Users cannot create their own.
6. **Sales reps cannot be iTOP Instructors** unless dental professionals actively working with patients.
7. **Shorter events not meeting minimum criteria cannot be called iTOP.**
8. **Events with different content cannot be called iTOP.**

## Logo & Brand Rules

- iTOP logo may only be used on iTOP marketing materials
- Can be used together with the Curaden Academy brand
- **DO NOT MIX iTOP and CURAPROX branding** — iTOP should look professionally independent
- The connection to CURAPROX should be clear but not dominant
- Locally produced material requires Academy Department approval before distribution
